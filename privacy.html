Reporting Practice app

The "Reporting Practice" app is a private tool operated by Clayton Cole. It requests read-only access to Google Analytics and Google Ads data in accounts that Clayton Cole owns or administers, in order to produce weekly performance reports. It does not request access to any other user's data.

  • Data retrieved is stored only on the operator's own computer and is used only to produce those reports.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Summarized numbers may be sent to an AI model provider to write the report's narrative; raw account credentials are never sent.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
